Why Chain Spa

Urban cyclists deserve a clean drivetrain.

More people are commuting by bike, riding gravel, and buying really nice bikes — but most city apartments don't come with a garage, a yard, or a hose. Chain Spa brings proper care where people ride: transit hubs, workplaces, trailheads, neighborhoods.

A clean drivetrain lasts twice as long. We want to make that easy.
